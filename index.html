What is Corner?

Corner is a global furniture brand making premium, designer-quality kitchens accessible to modern homeowners. We combine Scandinavian design principles, transparent pricing, and a streamlined remote process to deliver high-end kitchens without traditional luxury markups.

Our kitchens live in homes across 10+ US states and 5+ European countries. We sell directly to homeowners, design every kitchen ourselves, and build our own tools and playbooks.

How does Corner work?
  1. 01A homeowner finds us online and books a call with one of our sales designers in Brazil. Together they design the kitchen and agree on the price.
  2. 02Our technical designers turn the design into production drawings, checked against our standards to the millimetre.
  3. 03Our factory partner in Ukraine makes every cabinet to order. Our project managers ship it across the ocean and organise delivery and installation, and the client hears from us at every step.

We try new ideas in weeks, not quarters, and when something isn’t working, we fix it instead of explaining it.
