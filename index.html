What is CORNER?

Corner is a global furniture brand making premium, designer-quality kitchens accessible to modern homeowners. We combine Scandinavian design principles, transparent pricing, and a streamlined remote process to deliver high-end kitchens without traditional luxury markups.

Our kitchens live in homes across 12 US states and 5 European countries. We sell directly to homeowners, design every kitchen ourselves, and build our own tools and playbooks. Our designers and project managers stay with every kitchen from the first sketch to the last panel going in, and the date we promise a client is the date we work to.

How does Corner work?
  1. 01A homeowner finds us online and books a call with one of our sales designers in Brazil. Together they design the kitchen and agree on the price.
  2. 02Our technical designers turn the design into production drawings, checked against our standards to the millimetre.
  3. 03Our factory partner in Ukraine makes every cabinet to order. Our project managers ship it across the ocean and organise delivery and installation, and the client hears from us at every step.

We try new ideas in weeks, not quarters, and when something isn’t working, we fix it instead of explaining it.

Who we hire

  • Senior by output, not title. You’ve done real work for real clients, and you can show it: the kitchen you sold, the shipment you moved, the drawing that went to production.
  • Genuinely hungry. You want hard problems and visible results. You’re not here to fill time.
  • Self-directed. You don’t wait for direction. You read the brief, ask the right questions and get moving.
  • Precise. You notice the details others miss, and you don’t promise what you haven’t confirmed.
  • In it for the long run. You want to grow with a company for years, not collect a line on your CV.

Experience helps, but the most important thing is that you have the desire and energy to make a difference.
