Corner is a global kitchen brand making premium, designer-quality kitchens accessible to modern homeowners. We combine Scandinavian design principles, transparent pricing, and a streamlined remote process to deliver high-end kitchens without traditional luxury markups. Corner projects live in homes across 12 US states and 5 European countries.

The role

Every month we photograph a newly delivered Corner kitchen. Those images show how our kitchens live in real homes, and they’re often the exact picture that makes a future client say “I want one just like that.”

We’re hiring a Content & Communications Lead to own what happens to that material: getting it to editors, feeds, web pages and sales conversations, where it turns strangers into clients. You’ll report to our CEO and work closely with our Content Manager. You own the plan and the results, and you do much of the work yourself.

What you’ll own

  • Photoshoots. Brief the photographer and write the shot list for every shoot, coordinate with the homeowners, direct on the day, choose the selects and give retouching feedback.
  • Collection launches. Turn market trends, client calls and quiz data into a short insight brief, brief our 3D artists on renders, run the review rounds and plan each launch.
  • PR. Write the release and photo kit for each project, build the editor list, pitch named editors with a specific angle, follow up, and track coverage.
  • Instagram and Pinterest. Rebuild our highlights and posting strategy, plan the monthly calendar, curate and approve posts, and read the results every month to decide what changes.
  • Sales assets. Keep catalogs, project videos and presentation slides current, and build the reference library our designers use on calls: delivered projects by style, layout, size and price.
  • Website content. New project pages, collection pages and trust pages that explain our process, reviews and delivery.
  • Experiments. Creator and influencer outreach, new content built from what clients ask about, and new product-page formats.

What success looks like

  • Every shoot becomes a full set of assets within a month: a project page, Pinterest variants, short videos, a press pitch round and updated sales material.
  • Editors at design and home publications know Corner and open our emails.
  • Our designers bring a matching reference to every client call.
  • In your first 90 days: a working monthly content system, a rebuilt Instagram, two press campaigns sent and the reference library live.

About you

  • 3+ years in content, communications or PR, including at least 2 years owning content or communications at a design, interiors, furniture or lifestyle brand.
  • You’ve directed photo shoots, shipped web pages and pitched editors yourself, and you can show it.
  • Excellent written English for a US audience: press releases, pitches, captions and page copy.
  • A trained eye for interiors photography and renders: light, materials, composition.
  • Comfortable reading analytics: GA4, UTMs, Instagram insights.
  • Comfortable working with US time zones.
  • You plan your own work, report on it and finish what you start.
  • Preferable: experience with US design and home media, or at a kitchen, furniture or architecture brand.

You’ll work with

Our CEO, our Design Lead, our Content Manager, the sales designers, and the photographers and 3D artists we work with on each project.

Who we’re looking for

We hire people who think long-term, take ownership, and want to grow inside a fast-moving, performance-driven company. You should be comfortable with responsibility, structured processes and measurable outcomes. We value clarity, initiative, and the ability to move projects forward without constant supervision.
