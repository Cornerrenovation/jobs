Brand & Content Marketing Manager

RemoteFull-time2+ yearsReports to the CEO
Apply for this role

Corner is a global kitchen brand making premium, designer-quality kitchens accessible to modern homeowners. We combine Scandinavian design principles, transparent pricing, and a streamlined remote process to deliver high-end kitchens without traditional luxury markups. Corner projects live in homes across 12 US states and 5 European countries.

The role

We’re looking for a creative and ambitious Brand & Content Marketing Manager to own how Corner feels: someone who tells our story in pictures and in words, in a way people don’t just see and read, but feel.

We now photograph a delivered kitchen every month. Those images show how our kitchens live in real homes, and they’re often the exact picture that makes a future client say “I want one just like that.” The library is growing faster than anyone can put it to work. That’s this job.

You’ll play a key role in building the Corner brand across every channel, and you’ll work closely with our founder on strategy and direction. You’re responsible for making sure everything Corner shows and writes is sharp, beautiful, honest and engaging, and that it reaches editors, feeds, web pages and sales conversations, where it turns strangers into clients.

About you

You’re interested in design and have a high aesthetic sense. You notice furniture everywhere: in friends’ homes, in hotels, in films.

You’re thorough, detail-oriented and have a strong eye. You know the difference between a beautiful kitchen photo and the one that makes a stranger book a call.

  • You can create a project page that gets a US homeowner to book a call.
  • A keen eye for detail in interiors photography and renders: light, materials, composition.
  • 2+ years in content marketing, branding, social media or PR, ideally at a design, interiors, furniture or lifestyle brand.
  • Comfortable reading analytics: GA4, UTMs, Instagram insights.
  • Comfortable working with US time zones.
  • Preferable: experience with US design and home media, or at a kitchen, furniture or architecture brand.

Experience helps, but the most important thing is that you have the desire and energy to make a difference.

What you’ll own

Every month you direct a shoot. Every week you publish.

  • Photoshoots. Brief the photographer and write the shot list for every shoot, coordinate with the homeowners, direct on the day, choose the selects and give retouching feedback.
  • Collection launches. Turn market trends, client calls and quiz data into a short insight brief, brief our 3D artists on renders, run the review rounds and plan each launch.
  • PR. Write the release and photo kit for each project, build the editor list, pitch named editors with a specific angle, follow up, and track coverage.
  • Instagram and Pinterest. Rebuild our highlights and posting strategy, plan the monthly calendar, curate and approve posts, and read the results every month to decide what changes.
  • Sales assets. Keep catalogs, project videos and presentation slides current, and build the reference library our designers use on calls: delivered projects by style, layout, size and price.
  • Website content. New project pages, collection pages and trust pages that explain our process, reviews and delivery.
  • Experiments. Creator and influencer outreach, new content built from what clients ask about, and new product-page formats.

It’s a broad role for one person; we set priorities together each month. You don’t shoot; we work with photographers and 3D artists.

What success looks like

  • Every shoot becomes a full set of assets within a month: a project page, Pinterest variants, short videos and updated sales material.
  • Our designers bring a matching reference to every client call.
  • In your first 90 days: a content loop that turns each shoot into new client inquiries, a rebuilt Instagram and the reference library live.

You’ll work with

Our founders, our Design Lead, our Content Manager, the sales designers, and the photographers and 3D artists we work with on each project.

How we work

  • Ownership. We take ownership of our work as a matter of course. We set high standards for ourselves, and we expect the same from you.
  • Can-do. We see possibilities before problems. Our motto is: “I have never tried that before, so I think I should definitely be able to do that.”
  • Detail. We build our kitchens to the millimetre, and every photo and page we put out gets the same care.

What we offer

  • See your work convert: clients send us our own photos and ask for that kitchen.
  • A real chance to put a clear mark on Corner as a brand.
  • Lots of responsibility, freedom and room to grow, professionally and personally.
  • A place in an innovative, entrepreneurial company that’s set on becoming the world’s #1 furniture brand.
