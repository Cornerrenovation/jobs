Brand & Graphic Designer

Remote, South America or UkraineFull-time2+ yearsReports to the CEO
Apply for this role

Corner is a global furniture brand making premium, designer-quality kitchens accessible to modern homeowners. We combine Scandinavian design principles, transparent pricing, and a streamlined remote process to deliver high-end kitchens without traditional luxury markups. Corner projects live in homes across 10+ US states and 5+ European countries.

The role

We’re looking for a Brand & Graphic Designer who makes how Corner looks with their own hands: the photos, pages and posts people see long before they talk to us.

We photograph a delivered kitchen every month. You take each shoot from raw files to finished work. You pick the frames, retouch and crop them, and turn them into project pages, posts, catalogs and sales material that all look like one brand.

You’ll play a key role in how Corner looks everywhere, and you’ll work closely with our founder, who sets the direction.

About you

You’re interested in design and have a high aesthetic sense. You notice furniture everywhere: in friends’ homes, in hotels, in films.

You’re thorough, detail-oriented and have a strong eye. A crooked line in a photo bothers you before anyone else sees it.

  • 2+ years designing for a brand: in-house at a consumer brand, or at an agency or studio working on brand identities.
  • You know what keeps a brand consistent: its rules, its message, its look and feel. You have helped create at least one identity.
  • You work with photos yourself: selects, retouching, colour, crops, layouts.
  • You build pages, you don’t only mock them up. You work in Figma, Photoshop or Lightroom, InDesign or Illustrator, and Webflow or another site builder.
  • Spoken and written English for daily work.
  • Preferable: work for interiors, furniture, architecture or lifestyle brands, and shooting photo or short video yourself.

Experience helps, but the most important thing is that you have the desire and energy to make a difference.

What you’ll make

Every month a shoot comes in. Every week something new goes out.

  • Shoots. Build the shot list with us, give the photographer the visual brief, review on the day, choose the selects, then retouch, colour and crop.
  • Web pages. New project pages, collection pages and updates on our Webflow site, built from our components.
  • Instagram and Pinterest. Posts, carousels, covers and short video edits.
  • Sales and launch material. Catalogs, presentation slides, price sheets and visuals for new collections.
  • Our brand system. Our type, colours, image style and templates in one place, applied everywhere and grown as we grow.

You’ll work with

Our founders, our Sales & Design Lead, our Content Manager, the sales designers, and the photographers and 3D artists we work with on each project.

How we work

  • Ownership. We take ownership of our work as a matter of course. We set high standards for ourselves, and we expect the same from you.
  • Can-do. We see possibilities before problems. Our motto is: “I have never tried that before, so I think I should definitely be able to do that.”
  • Detail. We build our kitchens to the millimetre, and every photo and page we put out gets the same care.

What we offer

  • See your work convert: clients send us our own photos and ask for that kitchen.
  • A real chance to shape how a growing brand looks.
  • Lots of responsibility, freedom and room to grow, professionally and personally.
  • A place in an innovative, entrepreneurial company that’s set on becoming the world’s #1 furniture brand.

Designer gráfico, diretor de arte, diseñador gráfico or director de arte with brand work: this is for you too.
